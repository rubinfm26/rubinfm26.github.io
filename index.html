Our Purpose

Shared Models for a Shared Sky

Foundation models are trained once. This hack-week puts them in everyone's hands.

The Vera C. Rubin Observatory alert stream has been public since February 2026, delivering roughly 10 million alerts per night. Foundation models are the most powerful tools modern AI offers for data at this scale, but training one takes curated data, GPU time, and specialist engineering that no single research group can marshal. Over the past two years, teams at the NSF-Simons SkAI Institute built SELDON and StarEmbed, foundation models for multi-band light curves and variable stars, now consolidating into TEMPI, SkAI's world-model program for the time-domain sky.

This hack-week shares those models with the broader Rubin community. Working inside Hyrax, the LINCC Frameworks tool for model-agnostic exploration of survey data, we will compute and publish a shared latent-space representation of live Rubin alert-stream sources. It is the first time a SkAI foundation model runs at survey scale on real, operationally interrupted LSST observations rather than smooth simulated cadence.

On that shared representation, participants build independent downstream projects: transient classification, photometric redshifts, anomaly search, galaxy mergers, and catalog cross-matching with LSDB. The code and tutorials go back to the public Hyrax repository and the Rubin Data Academy. Supported by the LSST Discovery Alliance, the hack-week runs November 4–6, 2026 at the SkAI Institute in Chicago.

What We'll Build

Hack-Week Goals

By Friday: a working deployment of SkAI foundation models on real Rubin data, inside a framework the whole community can use

01

Shared Latent Space of Live Rubin Alerts

Compute SELDON and StarEmbed embeddings for live alert-stream sources and publish them inside Hyrax as a resource the whole Rubin community can use.

02

Downstream Science Probes

Train lightweight classifiers, photometric-redshift estimators, and anomaly searches on the frozen representation, so each project needs far fewer labels.

03

Simulation-to-Real Validation

Measure how models trained on ELAsTiCC simulations perform against Rubin's real operating cadence, with DP2 imaging as a static cross-check.

04

Catalog Cross-Matching at Scale

Use LSDB/HATS through the SCiMMA cross-match service to pull archival photometry and DES postage stamps for candidates flagged in the latent space.

05

Code, Tutorials, and Merged Pull Requests

Contribute working code back to the public Hyrax repository and write tutorial notebooks for the Rubin Data Academy.

06

Community Paper and Early-Career Training

Outline a community paper, and send early-career researchers from DESC, TVSSC, and ISSC home able to apply these tools to their own science.
