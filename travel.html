Everything you need to know for your visit to Evanston and Chicago

Transportation

Getting to Evanston

By Air

O'Hare International Airport (ORD) is the closer airport to Evanston, about 15 miles (25 km) away. Midway International Airport (MDW) is on the far side of Chicago, about 25 miles (40 km) away.

By Train

Chicago Union Station is served by Amtrak with connections from across the country. From Union Station, walk to Ogilvie Transportation Center and take the Metra Union Pacific North line to Davis Street, Evanston (about 25 minutes); CIERA is a 5-minute walk from the station.

By Car

Chicago is accessible via I-90/94 (Kennedy/Dan Ryan Expressway) from the north and south, and I-290 (Eisenhower Expressway) from the west. Evanston is reached via I-94 (Edens Expressway) to Dempster Street. There are public garages in downtown Evanston within a few blocks of CIERA, and we suggest the CTA Purple Line if you are staying in Chicago.

Airport to CIERA

From O'Hare: Taxis and rideshare take about 30 minutes (roughly $45–60). By transit, take the CTA Blue Line to Clark/Lake, the Red Line north to Howard, and the Purple Line to Davis, then walk 5 minutes to 1800 Sherman Ave. From Midway: Take the Orange Line to Roosevelt, the Red Line north to Howard, and the Purple Line to Davis (about 90 minutes), or a taxi (roughly $60–75).

About Evanston and Chicago

Make the most of your visit

Dining

Downtown Evanston has plenty of restaurants and cafes within a few blocks of CIERA, and Chicago's dining scene (deep-dish pizza to Michelin-starred restaurants) is 30–40 minutes away on the Purple and Red Lines.

Attractions

Walk the Northwestern lakefront campus, or head into Chicago for the Art Institute, Field Museum, Adler Planetarium, and Millennium Park. The SkAI Institute is in the John Hancock Center on Michigan Avenue, one Purple Line ride from Evanston.

Weather

Early December in Evanston is cold and often windy off the lake. Expect temperatures between 25–40°F (−4–4°C), and snow is likely, so pack a warm coat, hat, and boots.
