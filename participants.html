Applications

Applications Open Soon

We will convene roughly 25 to 35 participants. The expert core is already in place (the SkAI model builders, the Hyrax and LINCC Frameworks engineers, and the broker teams from ANTARES, Pitt-Google, and BOOM), and the open call fills the project teams around them with scientists from DESC, TVSSC, and ISSC. The call will go out through LSST Discovery Alliance and Science Collaboration Slack channels.

If you are a graduate student or postdoc comfortable with Python and light-curve data, please do apply! No prior experience with foundation models is needed. Priority goes to early-career researchers from smaller institutions who might otherwise be unable to attend. There is no registration fee, meals during the hack-week are provided, and we have travel and lodging support for out-of-town participants (see Travel & Lodging).
